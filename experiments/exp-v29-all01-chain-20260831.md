## Interpretation
**Failure mode:** `claim_false`

The label-head interaction poison is NOT dose-linear: it survives at the 0.1 weight that is provably safe for affect alone. affect 0.1 by itself gives eff_rank 8.599 (v28, the best arm on the books); adding diary 0.1 + context 0.1 on top drops it to 6.783, a -1.816 loss, comparable to the -1.50 exp-v22 measured with the same three heads at 0.3. So lowering the dose is exhausted as a lever and the next move on utilisation is ARCHITECTURAL, not a weight: detached probes (stop-gradient at the label-head inputs, so the heads read the representation instead of formatting it). Second lesson, methodological: this arm changed TWO variables at once (diary AND context), so it cannot say which head carries the damage -- the untested cells are diary-only-in-chain and context-only-in-chain, since v26 measured those for rank only. Third: the A/B geometry gate paid for itself here, costing 21 GPU-minutes instead of the ~93 a full chain burns, and the honest verdict is 'inconclusive' rather than 'refutes' because the deployment gates were never reached.
