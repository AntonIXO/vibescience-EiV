## Key claims
- Whole-trial emotion labels inject temporal label noise; peak-centered immediate-recall annotation (4s event windows) beats whole-trial labels sliced into segments by +3.8pp mean across 8 EEG classifiers (38.5% vs 34.7%), up to +4.8pp for stronger models (MAET 46.1 vs 41.3).
- Fine-grained annotation beats whole-trial by +9.7pp accuracy despite LESS training data - annotation precision beats data volume.
- Physiological validation: high-arousal peaks show ~91% SCR rate vs 6-12% for low-arousal.
- External support for sub-window (per-patch) state supervision over window-pooled argmax - the A1 patch arm's premise.
