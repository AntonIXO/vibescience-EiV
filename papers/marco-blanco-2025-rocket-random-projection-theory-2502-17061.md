## Key claims
- ROCKET's untrained random convolutional kernels are random projections in the compressed-sensing sense: they PRESERVE discriminative patterns of the time series with no training, and the PPV non-linearity captures natural sparsity; translation invariance and noise robustness are proved.
- Consequence for the random-twin control: an untrained patch encoder + linear/GBM readout is a strong baseline BY CONSTRUCTION, so trained ≈ random on a label means the training objective was not aligned with that label — not that the label is unreadable. EiV's Phase A objective aligns to HR-statistical captions; the encoder learned HR (PL60 gain is fully learned) and not state.
