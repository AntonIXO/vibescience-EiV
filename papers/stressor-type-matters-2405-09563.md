## Key claims
- Cross-dataset HRV stress models generalise when the STRESSOR TYPE matches; stress intensity and sensor brand had minimal impact. Four public datasets (WESAD, SWELL-KW, ForDigitStress, VerBIO).
- DIRECT CONSTRAINT ON OUR CORPUS PLAN: it predicts that pooling short induced-stress cohorts of MIXED stressor type (WESAD social-evaluative TSST vs CASE film-elicited affect vs nurse_stress occupational shift) will NOT transfer, even after the extent problem is fixed. Any 'buy more short-session corpora' plan (StressID/SWELL-KW/MAUS/POPANE) must group by stressor type, not merely by availability.
- This is the honest counterweight to the supply argument: fixing extent is necessary but the paper says stressor-type harmonisation is a SEPARATE and possibly binding constraint.
