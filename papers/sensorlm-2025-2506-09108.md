## Key claims
- 59.7M hours / 103k people of [26 features x 1440 min] Fitbit/Pixel days; ViT patch encoder; CoCa-style contrastive + captioning; the text decoder is a small multimodal decoder, NOT a frozen multi-billion LLM. No LLM-vs-head ablation is reported anywhere in the paper.
- Caption-type ablation (Table 4): semantic captions (events with minute spans, logged mood at minute t) are what make zero-shot concept readout work (0.71–0.84 AUROC vs 0.49–0.51 with statistical/structural only); statistical captions are what help the health-state linear probes (Anxiety 0.67–0.68 vs 0.63–0.65). Adding statistical to semantic HURTS zero-shot activity.
- Architecture ablation (Table 5): CoCa (contrastive + captioning) > CLIP > Cap; captioning-only has no usable projection for zero-shot. Generic LLMs (Gemini 2.0, Gemma-3-27B) given the day as tabular text are at chance (AUROC 0.50–0.55) on every sensor task.
- Diminishing returns past ~12M hours; undertrained XL is worse than B.
