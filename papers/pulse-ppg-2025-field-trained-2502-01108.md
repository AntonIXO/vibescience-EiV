## Key claims
- PPG foundation model trained ONLY on raw field data (100-day study, 120 participants, uncurated); generalises better than a clinical-data FM across lab and field tasks, and field pretraining beats clinical pretraining on many tasks.
- Supports the EiV corpus design principle that field variability (LifeSnaps, PMData, nurse_stress) is an asset for generalisation, and Amin 2025's finding that device/deployment quality dominates transfer.
