## Key claims
- Decoupled multimodal tokenizer with modality-INVARIANT (shared) and modality-SPECIFIC (private) codebooks over EEG/ECG/EOG/EMG; masked pretraining with both objectives; resilient fine-tuning with prototype alignment so any modality subset works at inference.
- SOTA on emotion recognition, sleep staging, motor prediction and mental workload with strong robustness to missing modalities -- a second independent instance (with PULSE) of the shared/private split being what makes a privileged or missing modality usable, and the design Track E adopts.
